I am **Dr. Jiawen Wang**, a postdoctoral researcher at **Soochow University**. My research lies at the intersection of **computational chemistry** and **artificial intelligence (AI)**, with a primary focus on the **rational design of core enzymes** for industrial biocatalysis.

I am deeply interested in **bridging the gap between theoretical calculations and experimental validation** — a central challenge in modern enzyme engineering. My work integrates:

- **Multi-scale molecular dynamics (MD) simulations** and **QM/MM** calculations to decipher the underlying catalytic mechanisms at the atomic level.
- **Generative AI models** for sequence and structure generation, enabling *de novo* enzyme design with enhanced catalytic efficiency and stability.
- **High-throughput screening** and multi-objective optimization pipelines to accelerate the transition from computational predictions to experimental reality.

My long-term goal is to build a **unified, AI-driven platform for enzyme design** that can be applied to a wide range of industrial and environmental challenges, including the degradation of PET plastics and other recalcitrant pollutants. I am always open to discussions, collaborations, and opportunities to connect with fellow researchers in the fields of **AI for Science, protein engineering, and computational biology**.
