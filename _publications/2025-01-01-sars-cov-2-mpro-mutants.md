This study focuses on the SARS-CoV-2 main protease (Mpro) mutants, combining molecular dynamics (MD) simulations and machine learning (ML) techniques to elucidate the key structural and dynamic factors underlying their altered activity. Our work provides valuable insights into the molecular mechanisms of drug resistance and lays the groundwork for future rational drug design.
